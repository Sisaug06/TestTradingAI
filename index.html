Directional rules: price + OI rising supports LONG participation; price falling + OI rising supports SHORT participation; falling OI with rising price is treated as short covering; falling OI with falling price as long liquidation. Extreme positive funding / long-holder crowding is a bearish headwind, while extreme negative funding / short-holder crowding is a bullish headwind. Basis is treated as confirmation when moderate and as a crowding warning when extreme. LOW liquidity reduces conviction.
FA Score is a decision score, not probability. “Fundamental Analysis” here means crypto-native market/derivatives fundamentals available from Bybit. External news, token unlocks, treasury data, on-chain activity and protocol fundamentals are intentionally not claimed.
